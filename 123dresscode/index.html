Who We Are

Fashion That Fits
Your Life

123 Dress Code was born out of one simple belief: every person deserves to look and feel extraordinary. We curate, tailor, and deliver clothing that moves with your life — whether that's closing a deal or opening the weekend.

Based in Nairobi, we serve clients across Kenya with a carefully selected range of men's and women's fashion, from everyday casual to occasion-ready formal wear.
